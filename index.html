Who We Are

True to the Art

At True Jiu Jitsu, we believe in getting back to what matters — the art itself. Whether you're a complete beginner looking to learn self-defense, a seasoned competitor chasing podiums, or someone who just wants a challenging workout, our mats are your home.

We offer Gi and NoGi Jiu Jitsu for all levels, plus personal fitness and striking classes. Our approach emphasizes technique, respect, and real-world effectiveness — not ego.

Our Programs

Gi & NoGi Jiu Jitsu

All-levels and advanced classes in both Gi and NoGi. Build your ground game from fundamentals to competition-ready technique.

Striking

Develop stand-up skills with focused striking classes. Learn practical techniques for self-defense and well-rounded martial arts.

Personal Fitness

Push your limits with personalized fitness training. Build strength, conditioning, and endurance to complement your training.
